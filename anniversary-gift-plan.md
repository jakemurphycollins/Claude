# First Anniversary Gift Plan

## Problem Statement
You want a first-anniversary gift that feels specifically designed for your wife and your relationship — not something you could hand to any couple. The meaningful anchors are your Mexico City honeymoon, a shared love of the Red Sox, and the reality that she's grinding through medical school (making your time together genuinely scarce). She values experiences, thoughtful personal touches, and quality time. The challenge isn't budget or creativity — it's converting intent into a concrete plan before decision paralysis turns it last-minute again.

## Chosen Approach
**Expandable Photo Album + Professionally Framed Honeymoon Print.**

The album is the living anchor: a high-quality, naturally expandable photo album (not a printed book — a physical album she can keep adding to herself, with no designer required and no visual inconsistency). It opens with the Mexico City honeymoon; the Ireland trip becomes the next chapter she adds. The framed print is the permanent piece: one standout honeymoon photo, professionally framed, that lives on a wall.

Together: the album grows with your marriage; the framed print is a fixed, beautiful artifact of where it started.

## Open Questions
- Which honeymoon photo is the one? (Needs to be chosen before framing is ordered)
- What's the album format that allows clean expansion — slip-in pockets, magnetic pages, or lay-flat with loose inserts?
- Does the album need a dedicated spot for written notes (captions, dates) or just photos?
- Frame style: does she lean modern/minimal, classic/ornate, or natural/warm wood?
- Framing vendor: local shop vs. Framebridge vs. Simply Framed?
- Photo printing: does the album need pre-printed photos ordered, or does she add her own prints over time?

## Out of Scope
This solution explicitly will NOT:
- Replace or compete with the Ireland trip
- Be a one-time printed book that can't be added to without professional help
- Be a digital gift (screen, frame app, etc.) — this is physical and permanent
- Address the medical school support angle (valid idea, different occasion)

## MVP
The minimum version that still fully lands: the expandable album, thoughtfully assembled with honeymoon photos and a handwritten note framing Ireland as Year 2, plus the framed print delivered before July 12th. Both pieces need to arrive on time — the album without the print (or vice versa) is still a complete gift if timing forces it, but the intention is both together.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Album format is naturally expandable — she can add photos herself without it looking patchy
- [ ] Album opens with Mexico City chapter and includes a written note framing Ireland as what's next
- [ ] Framed print is professionally matted and framed (not a drugstore print in an IKEA frame)
- [ ] Both pieces ready before July 12th
- [ ] She doesn't feel like this was thrown together (because it wasn't)
